How the reports were split

  1. By tenant, not by intake tag. A report’s cause depends on where the tenant is and which booking form they run (causes.ts). The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser.
  2. hint is any day-first tenant’s report that came in through the booking-v3 form, which prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says; the form reads it day first. Fixing the parser does not fix the hint.
  3. shift is any report whose body describes a move by one day — 12 May shown as 11 May, Tuesday shown as Monday, Lisbon and Toronto seeing different days. A day/month swap can never do that, so these are not the parser bug, whatever tenant sent them. The desk had already noted them (desk-notes.json) but counted them under parser; problems.ts reads the body and keeps them apart.
